Premium Lenses Guide

Overview

Having cataract surgery is a once-in-a-lifetime opportunity to completely transform how you see the world. In the past, patients were only offered one standard type of lens. Today, utilizing high-precision micro-technology, we offer a complete portfolio of "Premium Lenses." These advanced implants are designed to seamlessly integrate with your eye and significantly reduce your daily dependence on glasses.

Your Premium Options Include:

  • Toric Lenses: Correction to counteract astigmatism, ordered based on your level of astigmatism.
  • EDOF Lenses: A blend of distance and intermediate vision and the ability to read large print. Great for active, modern lifestyle with reduced risk of haloes and glare compared to a multifocal (trifocal lens).
  • Trifocal Lens (multifocal lens): This aims to give you the greatest reduction in spectacle dependence, covering distance, middle and near (reading) vision. Requires time to adapt to with risks of haloes, glare and starbursts.
  • Enhanced Monofocal Lens: Aims to give you excellent distance vision, whilst extending the range to also give you intermediate "arm's length" vision with significantly less risk of experiencing glare and night time vision issues.
